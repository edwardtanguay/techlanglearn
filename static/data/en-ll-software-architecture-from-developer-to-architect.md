## Big picture vs. little picture, 04:27, 2025-02-18

- moving from developer to architect
  - scope of responsibilities widens
  - no longer have the freedom to focus on a single aspect of an application
  - focusing on a component is important to a developer but not for an architect
  - need to know how each part fits into the whole
  - like a conductor of an orchestra
  - have a rudimentary knowledge of how they all work
  - need to know the impact of a change to a component within the rest of the application
  - get developers on board to understand why changes are necessary
  - focus on wide-ranging interaction

## Teacher rather than dictator, 3:31, 2025-02-18

- teacher not dictator
  - you don't need to solve all the problems
  - same skills of developer are not those of the architect
  - do not announce the answer
  - work with developers, what are the facts
  - guide developers
  - educate developers
  - motivate developers
  - architects are not hero developers but a catalyst

## Evaluator and feedback, 3:36, 2025-02-18

- evaluation and feedback
  - you are the one with a perspective of the overall system
  - reduce technical debt, the cost to support the application
  - know the customer well and guide the decisions of developers based on that
  - you decide or raise the visibility of the decision
  - you are a mentor for all engineers
  - technical coach
  - communicate the decisions to the team
  - more of a teacher than a decision maker

## Learn and experiment: Self, 4:04, 2025-02-18

- typical day of software architect
  - help solve immediate problems
  - step back to learn and grow her own architectural skills
    - write software that will not be used, to try out new ideas
    - experiment with machine learning
    - new algorithms
    - works with open source software
  - reading, learning
  - new processes, what other companies new
  - reads best practices
  - grows network with fellow architects

## Dev and ops: Both work together, 3:33, 2025-02-18

- devops
  - you are part of development and operations
  - how does it deal with high loads
    - brown out

## Scale and availability: Beyond just making an app, 2:56, 2025-02-18

- two important aspects: scalability and availability
  - particularly important if application is SaaS
    - Zendesk, Slack, Dropbox
  - scalability
    - BOOK: Architecting for Scale (Lee Atchison, teacher of this course)
    - risk management
    - service metrics
  - availability
    - most sensitive to problems when the most users are using it
    - monitoring
    - upgrades
